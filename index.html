COLLECTIVE BIO

Co-founded by Mourir Beau and Scarface, DYS is a hidden gem in Milan's underground — a collective born from a shared vision to bring raw energy, emotion, and connection back to the dance floor.

Their debut series, XXX, introduced a high-intensity yet intimate rave format built on one mission: to keep people dancing side by side, united by rhythm and energy until the very end. Since then, DYS has brought their signature frenzy to some of Milan's most iconic venues alongside carefully selected special guests.

Dedicated to showcasing the city's underground talent, Destroy Your Spirit is known for its wide spectrum of grooves and fearless approach to sound. Mourir Beau and Scarface often take the closing slot, sealing each night with a powerful crescendo — a chaotic, euphoric release that defines the DYS experience.

THE FORMAT

DYS understands the power of production, atmosphere, and aesthetic in transforming a night out into an experience. Their approach merges meticulous attention to sensory detail with a raw, emotional undercurrent — creating spaces where sound, light, and feeling collide.

The XXX format embodies a surreal, psychedelic blend of spirituality and esotericism, contrasted against a hyperreal portrait of youthful nightlife: romantic, chaotic, and unashamedly human. The aesthetic finds beauty in the underbelly — chemical connections, eroticism, lust, confusion, pride, thrill-seeking, and the restless search for meaning in modern times.

Through hypnotic visuals and trance-inducing lighting, Destroy Your Spirit dissolves the line between spirituality and hedonism. The music is fluid, genre-defying, and perfectly crescendoed — mirroring the chaos and beauty of the experience itself.
