COLLECTIVE BIO

DYS is three people out of Milan: Mourir Beau, Scarface and figo. They built it from nothing more than a night they wanted and couldn't find anywhere else.

It began as XXX, in small rooms with sound too big for them and one rule that has never moved: keep the floor full and everyone on it until the lights come up. Word travelled, and XXX grew into bigger rooms, drawing the kind of guests the three of them would queue for themselves.

The lineups lean on Milan's own underground, and the closing set is theirs, never once brought down gently.

THE FORMAT

Here, how a night feels counts for as much as who's playing. Everything in the room, the light included, points the same way, toward somewhere you disappear into rather than something you film.

XXX lives somewhere surreal and faintly occult, pressed against the rougher side of a night out, all appetite and chemistry and the 4am search for anything that means something, the ego dissolved somewhere on the way in. None of it gets cleaned up, and that's most of the appeal.

Spirit and indulgence aren't at odds in here. The visuals smudge the line on purpose, and the music moves with them, gathering through the night instead of spending itself early.
